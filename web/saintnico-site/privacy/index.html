Verdly helps you build habits with friends — streaks, challenges, duo accountability, and progress insights. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using Verdly, you agree to this Policy.

1. Who this Policy covers

This Policy applies to the Verdly Android app and related services we operate (including account sync, social features, referrals, and support). It does not cover third-party websites or services that we do not control.

2. Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: you provide it, the App generates it through use, and our service providers process it to run Verdly.

2.1 Account & profile

  • Authentication identifiers (for example Firebase user ID) and sign-in details from your chosen sign-in method
  • Display name, username, photo, motto, bio, and other profile fields you choose to add
  • Contact details you share with us for support

2.2 Habits, goals & progress

  • Habits, goals, completions, streaks, shields, focus sessions, XP, levels, and related progress data
  • Preferences such as reminders, theme, notification settings, and app configuration
  • Some of this data is stored on your device and may sync to our servers so your account works across sessions

2.3 Social & community features

  • Friend connections, friend requests, duo partnerships, nudges, and challenge membership
  • Public-facing profile elements needed for leaderboards, grove activity, challenges, and accountability
  • Reports you submit about other users, and limited context needed to review them
  • Referral codes and referral qualification status

2.4 Media & device permissions

  • Camera / photos: when you set a profile photo or submit challenge proof photos
  • Location: only when a feature needs it (for example location-linked proof) and only with your permission
  • Notifications: reminders, social activity, streaks, and similar alerts if you allow them

You can deny or revoke permissions in your device settings. Some features may not work without them.

2.5 Purchases & subscriptions

  • Purchase and subscription status for Verdly Pro / premium access
  • Billing is processed by Google Play and our subscription partner (RevenueCat). We do not receive or store your full payment card number

2.6 Diagnostics & product analytics

  • App usage events, feature engagement, crash/diagnostic signals, device/app version, and similar technical data
  • We use this to keep Verdly reliable, understand what works, and improve the product experience

3. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, personalize, and improve Verdly
  • Sync your habits and social features securely
  • Send reminders and service notifications you enable
  • Process subscriptions and unlock premium features
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, spam, and policy violations
  • Review user reports and enforce community safety
  • Communicate important service updates and respond to support requests
  • Comply with law and protect our rights, users, and the App

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

Depending on your location, we process information because:

  • It is needed to provide Verdly (contract / service delivery)
  • You gave consent (for example optional permissions)
  • We have a legitimate interest in security, product improvement, and preventing abuse, balanced against your rights
  • We must meet legal obligations

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only as needed with:

  • Service providers who help us run Verdly, such as Google Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Functions), Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat

  • Authorities or advisors, when required by law or to protect users, the App, or our rights
  • A successor, if we are involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer (with appropriate safeguards)

6. Data storage & security

We use reputable cloud providers and industry-standard safeguards (including encryption in transit where supported). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Please choose a strong account sign-in method and keep your device secure.

Habit and preference data may be stored locally on your device and/or in our cloud backend. Deleting the App from your device does not automatically delete your cloud account data.

7. Retention

We keep information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide Verdly, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, maintain security, and meet legal, tax, or accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it within a reasonable period, subject to backups and legal holds.
