Delete your account

Request permanent deletion of your Verdly account and associated personal data. This page meets Google Play’s account deletion requirements.

How to request deletion

Email us from the address linked to your Verdly account:

Email deletion request

Or write to cheoleo332@gmail.com with subject line: Verdly Account Deletion Request

What to include

  • The email address on your Verdly account
  • Your Verdly username (if you set one)
  • A clear request to permanently delete the account

What we delete

  • Account / authentication record
  • Profile (name, username, photo, bio, motto)
  • Habits, goals, streaks, progress, and synced app data linked to your account
  • Social links you created (where we control them), subject to legal/safety holds

What may remain for a limited time

  • Backups, security logs, or records we must keep for legal, fraud, or accounting reasons
  • Content that is solely on another user’s device or that we are required to retain

Timeline

We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of verifying your request (often sooner). We may contact you to confirm it’s really you.

In the app

You can also wipe local/cloud data from Verdly settings where available (for example reset / wipe account data). Email is the official web path for full account deletion requests.

Deleting the Verdly app from your phone does not delete your cloud account. Use this page (or the email above) to request full deletion.
